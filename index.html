02 / Problem-led building

I start by noticing friction in the real world, then work backwards toward a practical tool that makes it lighter.

01

Campus operations

Certificates should not be
an administrative burden.

At EMC, creating certificates and recording leadership recognition by hand was slow, repetitive, and difficult to verify later. I wanted to leave behind something useful for the students and teams that continue after us.

The response: EMC Veritas - a digital system for certificates, leadership recognition, and public verification.

02

Access to legal clarity

Founders need answers before
they can afford a lawyer.

Early-stage founders often face contracts and company documents without the budget for a costly first legal review. The challenge was not to replace counsel, but to give people a clearer first step at a fraction of that initial cost.

The response: Clarifyd - AI-assisted contract risk review, founder-friendly rewrites, and transparent limitations.

03

Student readiness

Preparation should not depend on
having the right material.

When the HEC test became an urgent concern for many students, a lot of them did not know where to begin or have enough quality material to prepare. The goal was to make meaningful practice available without adding another financial barrier.

The response: ByteScore - a free practice portal with mock tests, performance reports, and exam preparation support. Students using it achieved stronger outcomes, including scores up to the 91st percentile.
